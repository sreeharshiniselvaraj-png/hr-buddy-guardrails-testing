# Sample HR Policy for HR Buddy Testing
**Company reference:** TCS-style sample  
**Document status:** FICTIONAL SAMPLE — NOT AN OFFICIAL TCS POLICY  
**Purpose:** Test whether HR Buddy can retrieve policy information, answer directly, and cite the correct section.  
**Important:** Every rule and number below is invented for demonstration. Do not use it as real TCS guidance.

## 1. Leave Policy

### 1.1 Casual Leave Entitlement
For this sample test only, employees receive **12 casual leave days per calendar year**.

### 1.2 Eligibility and Rules
- This is fictional test data, not a verified company rule.
- Leave approval depends on manager approval in this sample workflow.
- Employees should check their individual balance through the official HR system; HR Buddy must not invent an employee's personal balance.

### 1.3 Leave Application Process
For this sample workflow:
1. Submit a leave request through the company's HR portal.
2. Select the leave type and requested dates.
3. Add a brief reason if the portal requires it.
4. Submit the request and wait for manager approval.
5. Check the HR portal for the request status.

### 1.4 Missing or Personal Information
HR Buddy must not guess individual leave balances or make decisions on behalf of a manager. If the real policy or an employee-specific record is unavailable, direct the employee to the official HR team.

## 2. Public Holiday Policy

### 2.1 Holiday Calendar
The approved holiday calendar must be obtained from the company's official internal calendar. **No actual holiday dates are provided in this sample**, so HR Buddy must not invent dates.

### 2.2 Holiday Updates
Employees should refer to the official internal calendar or contact HR if dates are missing or appear to have changed.

## 3. Reimbursement Policy

### 3.1 Eligible Expenses
Eligible expense categories must be confirmed from the official reimbursement policy. This sample does not define actual eligible categories.

### 3.2 Required Documents
Employees should follow the requirements listed in the official reimbursement portal or policy. HR Buddy must not invent document requirements.

### 3.3 Submission and Status
Employees should use the officially designated reimbursement system and check the claim status there. If the system or submission steps are not documented, HR Buddy should direct the employee to the official HR or Finance team.

### 3.4 Limits and Timelines
**No reimbursement limits, deadlines, or processing times are defined in this sample.** HR Buddy must not guess these details.

## 4. HR Contact
Use the company's verified internal HR contact. Do not assume an email address from this sample is valid for TCS.

## 5. HR Buddy Knowledge Rules
1. Answer using only the relevant documented section.
2. Cite the exact section used, such as **Section 1.1 — Casual Leave Entitlement**.
3. Do not present this fictional sample as official TCS policy.
4. Do not invent holiday dates, reimbursement limits, deadlines, or individual balances.
5. If official information is missing, clearly say it is unavailable and direct the employee to the verified HR contact.

## Suggested Knowledge QA Test
**Question:** How many casual leaves do I get per year?  
**Expected sample answer:** “In this sample policy, employees receive 12 casual leave days per calendar year (Section 1.1 — Casual Leave Entitlement). This is fictional test data, not an official TCS policy.”
